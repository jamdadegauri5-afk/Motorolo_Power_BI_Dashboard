# Motorolo_Power_BI_Dashboard
The Motorola Sales Dashboard analyzes sales performance using KPIs like total sales, quantity, transactions, and average sales. It visualizes trends by city, month, payment method, ratings, and mobile models to support data-driven business decisions.
## Dashboard
- <a href="https://github.com/jamdadegauri5-afk/Motorolo_Power_BI_Dashboard/blob/main/Motorolo_Power_BI_Dashboard.pbix">Dashboard</a>

## KPI’s REQUIREMENT

Total Sales: Measure overall revenue performance

Total Quantity Sold: Track number of products sold

Total Transactions: Count of all completed orders

Average Sales: Monitor average sales value per transaction

## CHARTS REQUIREMENT

Total Sales by City (Map Chart): Identify high-revenue cities

Total Quantity by Month (Line Chart): Analyze monthly sales trends

Customer Ratings (Bar Chart): Evaluate customer satisfaction levels

Transactions by Payment Method (Pie Chart): Understand payment preferences

## INSIGHTS

Major sales are generated from metro cities like Delhi, Mumbai, and Bangalore

Sales show consistent monthly trends with slight seasonal fluctuations

UPI and Debit Card are the most used payment methods

Customer ratings are mostly positive, with 5-star ratings dominating

Certain mobile models contribute significantly more to revenue

## CONCLUSION

This dashboard provides a clear and interactive view of Motorola sales performance. It helps analyze customer behavior, regional performance, product demand, and payment trends. The insights enable better business planning, targeted marketing, and improved decision-making.
